Big Five personality detection from natural language

A systematic literature review: how has the Big Five (five-factor) personality model been predicted from human language - written text and spoken language - and with what data, methods and results?

Open the explorer → An interactive page over the results extracted from the included papers. Chart a metric and trait, or what the papers use (dataset, model architecture, modality) over time; switch papers off and on and see the summary and charts change.

Using the explorer:

  • At the top, choose A result to look at a reported metric for a trait, or What the papers use to count datasets, model architectures or modalities, including over publication years.
  • Nothing you do on the page is saved; reload it to start again.
  • Each paper links to its source (DOI or publisher). The PDF links do not work on this site: they point at copies kept only with the review's own files.
  • The pooled estimates on the page use a simpler model than the review's analysis, so they will not match its reported figures exactly.
Made with LitRev, a systematic literature review tool. The review's configuration is in bnomis/litrev-big-five-nlp. Page generated 2026-11-15.
