### Needs attention: PAN 2015 lost its language from `split`

Liu et al. 2017 (`10.18653/v1/e17-1071`) reports English, Italian and Spanish
apart. Before this re-send its rows read dataset "PAN 2015" with the language
in `split` ("English, tweet-level"); now the language is in `dataset`
("PAN 2015 (English)") and `split` reads "tweet-level, 10-fold stratified
cross-validation". Merging the three into PAN-AP-2015 would make that paper's
three languages identical rows in every table and chart, hence the reject:
the three names are kept as written, at the cost of this paper counting under
them rather than under PAN-AP-2015. Its metric is RMSE, so no pooled analysis
is affected either way.

### Not for this review: `n` rule breaks

Two papers' `n` still comes from a sum of class counts, against the
tightened rule: `10.18653/v1/2025.realm-1.1` (for example 138 = 137 + 1) and,
very likely, `10.22266/ijies2021.0430.22`, whose `n_quote` is only a table
caption. Both are F1 or AUC rows, which no analysis pools.
