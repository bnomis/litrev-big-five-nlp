## Decisions

### Decision 1: generic dataset names

**What is being decided:** whether a generic table name such as "Ours" or
"News" may become an alias of the corpus its paper names elsewhere.

**Background:** a vocabulary alias is global, not per paper. On 2026-10-11
several papers print a generic name in their tables ("Ours" in
`s41597-025-04836-w`, which its datasets field calls BF-CC) and a specific
one in the datasets field. A name that is already an alias is never sent to
`normalise` again.

**What it affects:** `vocabularies/datasets.json`, and how the `dataset`
column labels these papers' rows in synthesis tables and charts.

**Options:**

- **Leave them unassigned (recommended).** The rows show the generic name
  as printed, e.g. "Ours". Nothing is grouped wrongly later.
- **Assign them.** These papers' rows join their corpus now. A later paper
  printing "Ours" or "News" is grouped into the same corpus silently, with
  no proposal to catch it.

The page carries this as the recommendation on each generic name; assigning
one there chooses the second option for that name.
